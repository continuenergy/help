Telling the Continu story

A reminder of who we're talking to and how the story moves, drawn from the sales proposal.

Who we're talking to

Operational Energy Users

Commercial and industrial, business-critical, multi-site and high energy users.

The challenges we solve
  • Power disruption. Outages, poor power quality and interruptions that stop production and create costly recovery.
  • Uncontrollable energy costs. High, unpredictable prices that squeeze margin and make budgeting harder.
  • Backup power complexity. Multiple systems and suppliers, limited visibility and unclear ownership.
How it feels across the business
  • Business owners: energy anxiety.
  • IT managers: ambient anxiety.
  • Maintenance managers: downtime dread.

The story anchors

01

Setting the scene

Show you understand their world. Recap their priorities, name the challenges we solve, and show how every industry is shifting through electrification, automation and digitalisation.

02

Conflict

Build the tension. Rising energy dependence, a built-in cost disadvantage, volatility, a grid that can't keep up, worsening power quality, the cost of downtime, and partial fixes that create bigger problems.

03

Big idea

The turning point: take control of your energy with one connected system that keeps power under control.

04

Resolution

Show how Continu makes it real: why Continu, the team, the site-wide approach and the five steps of the Endless Energy Accelerator.

05

Proof

Earn belief with awards, the power-critical sectors that trust Continu, our track record, case studies and clients' own words.

06

Close

Ask for one clear next step, such as a lunch and learn for their team.
